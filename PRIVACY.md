# Privacy policy — Snakes & Ladders (Jungle Edition)

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

**What we collect.** When you sign in, Firebase Authentication stores your
account identifier and, for Google sign-in, your display name, e-mail
address and profile picture. The game stores your chosen display name and
avatar, your match history (mode, players, turns, result) and aggregate
statistics (games, wins, rating) so you can see them across devices and on
the leaderboard. Online matches store the room, seats and every move while
the game runs and a summary afterwards.

**Guests.** Playing as a guest creates an anonymous account with no personal
details. Playing offline keeps everything on your device.

**What we don't do.** We do not sell data, show third-party ads or track you
across other apps or sites.

**Where it lives.** Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and
Realtime Database) for project `car-game-e403f` and processed by our
backend service, which validates matches and keeps leaderboards honest.

**Your choices.** You can change your display name and avatar in Profile and
sign out at any time. To delete your account and data, contact the address
below; deletion removes your profile, history and leaderboard entries.

**Contact.** Replace this line with your support e-mail before publishing.
